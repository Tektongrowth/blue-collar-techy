# Blue Collar Techy brand kit

Nick approved this as the primary logo on 2026-10-20. It is a clean vector redraw of the gear-B logo the site already used, with the same design, layout and colors. The old `logos/bct-logo-horizontal.png` built from a JPEG was a test and is gone. The April SVGs in `logos/` (`bct-primary*.svg`, `bct-horizontal.svg`, `bct-circular.svg`, `bct-mono-square.svg`, `bct-favicon.svg`) are an earlier design and are **not** the primary logo.

The one-color files use pure `#FFFFFF` and `#000000`. For print, treat colors as ink callouts. The closest Pantone for the orange still has to be chosen against a physical swatch; none has been picked yet.

## Fonts

The lettering is converted to outlines in every file, so no fonts are needed to use the logos.

- **BLUE COLLAR**: Titillium Web Bold Italic, slanted a little further (about 7.5° total) and stretched about 5% wider. SIL Open Font License 1.1.
- **TECHY**: Archivo ExtraBold (wght 800) at its Expanded width (wdth 125), upright with a 2° slant and stretched about 14% wider. SIL Open Font License 1.1.
- For matching text set beside the logo (signage, captions), use Titillium Web Bold Italic or Archivo ExtraBold.

## Usage rules

- Keep clear space around the logo at least the height of the B on every side.
- Minimum size: horizontal 1.25 in / 120 px wide; stacked 1 in / 90 px wide; mark 16 px.
- On dark garments and backgrounds use `fullcolor-dark`. On light ones use `fullcolor-light`. When one ink or thread color is needed, use the `1c-*` files.
- Don't stretch, rotate, outline, add shadows, recolor parts outside the files here, or rearrange the gear and wordmark.
- Use the hat icon and the round patch for embroidery. The other files are for print, screen and DTF.

### Embroidery notes

- **Hat icon** files are set to 2.25 in wide. At that size the narrowest parts are well over 1.5 mm: the gear ring is 5.2 mm, the B's middle bar 4.3 mm, and the gap between the B and the ring at least 2.4 mm. The B's rough, spiky left edge would not stitch cleanly, so in the hat icon and the patch the B has a straight left side. Everything is solid fill with no gradients or strokes.
- In the full-color hat icon the dark disc behind the B runs 4 units (about 1.1 mm) under the gear ring, so no gap shows when the stitches pull.
- **Round patch** (revised 2026-10-20 at Nick's request: gear-B icon about 18% smaller; BLUE COLLAR and TECHY about a third taller in a wider text band). At 3 in: BLUE COLLAR stems 1.6 mm, horizontal bars 1.3 mm; TECHY stems 2.3 mm, bars 1.5 mm. At 4 in: BLUE COLLAR 2.1 / 1.8 mm, TECHY 3.1 / 2.0 mm. Order it at **3.5 in or larger** for embroidery; at that size every stroke is at least 1.5 mm. At 3 in it works as a woven or printed patch, or embroidered if the digitizer accepts the 1.3 mm BLUE COLLAR bars. The inner ring and orange border are 1.5 mm or more at 3 in. The merrowed edge color is the digitizer's call (orange matches the border).
- **Horizontal or stacked lockup embroidered** (left chest and similar): the horizontal at 3.5 in wide has BLUE COLLAR stems of 1.6 mm and bars of 1.3 mm, so 4 in wide is the safe size. The stacked lockup at 3 in wide has 1.9 mm stems and 1.6 mm bars. Smaller than that, use the hat icon.
- The digitizer still makes the actual stitch file (DST/EMB) from these SVG or PDF files.
